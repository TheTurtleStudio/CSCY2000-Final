Dr. Riley McCurdy

Dean of Arcane Magic • Southern Hogwarts University

🔮

Arcane Scholar & Mentor

Dr. Riley McCurdy has served as Dean of Arcane Magic at Southern Hogwarts University for over twelve years. A prodigy in metaphysical spell construction, Dr. McCurdy earned their doctorate in Advanced Thaumaturgy from the Obsidian Athenaeum at the age of twenty-four—the youngest in the institution’s history.

Their research bridges classical incantation theory with modern chaos-magic frameworks, yielding breakthroughs in sustainable spell-weaving and ethical enchantment design. Under their leadership, the Arcane Studies department has become a beacon for innovators and traditionalists alike.

Teaching Philosophy

“Magic is not merely power—it is responsibility, precision, and poetry. I challenge my students not just to cast spells, but to understand why they work, how they shape reality, and what they reveal about the caster’s soul.”

Notable Achievements

  • Author of The Weave and the Word: Foundations of Ethical Spellcraft (2023)
  • Recipient of the International Order of the Silver Quill (2021)
  • Developed the "Luminous Syntax" curriculum adopted by 14 magical universities worldwide
  • Pioneer in non-verbal, intent-based arcana for neurodivergent practitioners

Courses Taught

  • ARC 401: Advanced Theoretical Thaumaturgy
  • ARC 550: Ethics of High Magic
  • ARC 602: Independent Research in Arcane Innovation
  • HON 399: The Philosophy of Magical Consequence
