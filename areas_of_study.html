ACADEMIC PATHS
👻

Ghoul Studies

Delve into the mysteries of the spectral realm, learning to commune with spirits and understand the delicate balance between life and death. Students master the art of protective wards, spirit communication, and the ethical treatment of supernatural entities.

Requirements:

  • Strong constitution and mental fortitude
  • Open mind and willingness to confront the unknown
  • Completion of Basic Magical Theory
  • Recommendation from a practicing spirit medium
💧

Water Magic

Harness the fluid power of water through advanced hydrokinetic techniques. Students learn to manipulate water in all its forms, from gentle streams to devastating tsunamis, while mastering the art of healing waters and aquatic communication.

Requirements:

  • Natural affinity for water and emotional sensitivity
  • Completion of Elemental Foundations
  • Ability to swim proficiently
  • Understanding of marine ecosystems
⚡

Thunder and Lightning

Command the raw power of the storm and lightning. Students learn to channel electrical energy, create protective barriers, and master the ancient art of storm-calling. This path requires exceptional focus and mental discipline.

Requirements:

  • High magical sensitivity and energy control
  • Completion of Advanced Energy Theory
  • Physical endurance and quick reflexes
  • Understanding of meteorological patterns
🔮

Arcane Studies

Master the fundamental forces of magic itself. Students explore the deepest mysteries of magical theory, learn to create new spells, and study the ancient texts that form the foundation of all magical knowledge. The most challenging and prestigious path.

Requirements:

  • Exceptional magical aptitude and intellectual curiosity
  • Completion of all core subjects with distinction
  • Proficiency in at least three magical disciplines
  • Approval from the Dean of Arcane Studies
